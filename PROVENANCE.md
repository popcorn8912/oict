# Provenance & Independence

OICT is an independent open-source project. This page states, for contributors and adopters, where its contents come from.

## What OICT is built from

- **Public material only.** The design draws on publicly available specifications and standards (for example RFC 2119, RFC 8174, OAuth 2.0, JSON Schema, and openly published agentic and universal commerce protocols) and on general software-engineering practice for idempotency and authorization.
- **Synthetic data only.** Every product, price, participant and order in this repository is invented. See [`src/core/fixtures.ts`](src/core/fixtures.ts).
- **Original code.** The reference implementation, conformance suite and benchmark were written for this project. They do not contain, derive from, or describe any employer's or client's source code, internal designs, internal documentation, data, or other confidential information.
- **Personal resources.** The project is developed on personal time with personal equipment and accounts.

## What OICT is not

- It is **not** affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any employer, retailer, game engine, platform, payment network or protocol owner.
- Product and protocol names appear only to describe interoperability. All trademarks belong to their owners.
- It is **not** a production payment system. See [Security considerations](spec/07-security-considerations.md).

## For contributors

Contributions must follow the same rules. By signing off your commits under the [Developer Certificate of Origin](CONTRIBUTING.md#developer-certificate-of-origin), you confirm that you have the right to submit the work and that it contains no confidential or proprietary material belonging to anyone else.
